# Employee Benefits & Workplace Policies Handbook

## 1. Retirement & 401(k) Savings Plan
The company partners with Fidelity Investments to provide a comprehensive 401(k) retirement savings plan designed to support long-term financial security for full-time and part-time employees.

### Company Matching Formula
- **Tier 1**: The company provides a dollar-for-dollar (100%) match on the first 4% of eligible compensation contributed by the employee.
- **Tier 2**: The company provides an additional 50% match on the next 2% of eligible compensation contributed.
- **Maximum Company Contribution**: Up to 5% of total eligible compensation when the employee contributes at least 6%.
- **Vesting Schedule**: All company matching contributions are subject to immediate 100% vesting upon deposit. There is zero waiting period or vesting cliff.
- Both Traditional pre-tax 401(k) and Roth post-tax 401(k) contribution options are available.

## 2. Paid Time Off (PTO) & Leave Policies
We believe rest, recovery, and time with family are essential for sustained creativity and high performance.

### Flexible Paid Time Off (PTO)
- Full-time employees accrue 25 days of paid time off per calendar year, accrued semi-monthly on the 15th and last day of each month.
- In addition to standard PTO, the company observes 12 paid corporate holidays each year, including Martin Luther King Jr. Day, Memorial Day, Juneteenth, Labor Day, Thanksgiving (Thursday and Friday), and Winter Break (December 24 through January 1).
- **Rollover Allowance**: Up to 5 unused PTO days may roll over into the following calendar year. Any additional accrued PTO exceeding 5 days is cashed out on December 31st.

### Paid Parental Leave Policy
- Primary and secondary caregivers are eligible for 16 consecutive weeks of fully paid parental leave following the birth of a child, legal adoption, or permanent foster placement.
- Parental leave may be taken at any time within the first 12 months following the qualifying event.
- Employees continue to accrue standard PTO and retain all active healthcare benefits throughout the duration of their parental leave.

## 3. Health, Wellness & Educational Allowances
Comprehensive healthcare coverage begins on the first calendar day of the month following the employee's date of hire.

### Health Insurance Premiums
- **Employee Coverage**: The company covers 100% of the monthly premium costs for all employees enrolled in our Gold PPO, Silver HDHP, or Kaiser Permanente HMO plans.
- **Dependent Coverage**: The company covers 75% of monthly healthcare premiums for eligible legal spouses, domestic partners, and dependent children under age 26.
- Dental and vision plans are 100% company-funded for both employees and their eligible dependents.
- Employees enrolled in the High Deductible Health Plan (HDHP) receive an annual company contribution of $1,000 (individual) or $2,000 (family) deposited directly into their Health Savings Account (HSA).

### Professional Development & Equipment Stipends
- **Annual Educational Stipend**: $2,500 per calendar year per employee to cover college tuition, industry conferences, books, online courses, and professional certifications.
- **Home Office Setup Allowance**: A one-time $1,000 tax-free reimbursement for remote and hybrid employees to purchase ergonomic desks, chairs, monitors, and peripherals.
- **Monthly Connectivity Reimbursement**: $75 per month added automatically to payroll to defray residential broadband internet and mobile cellular costs.
